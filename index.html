06 — Reflections

Learning Reflections

Key Insights
  • Accuracy and consistency in IT audits are critical — small discrepancies in asset records can affect compliance and decision-making.
  • Proper documentation and lifecycle tracking are crucial for organizational efficiency.
  • Systematic troubleshooting and preventive maintenance reduce downtime significantly.
  • Timely technical support directly impacts team productivity.
Challenges
  • Managing large volumes of asset data during audits was demanding, requiring patience and extreme attention to detail.
  • Some hardware issues were complex and required multiple diagnostic approaches before reaching a solution.
  • Balancing audit tasks, asset validation, and technical support simultaneously tested time management skills.
Problem-Solving
  • Developed a structured workflow for audits, breaking tasks into smaller steps for thorough validation.
  • Applied both built-in tools and third-party utilities for hardware diagnostics, cross-checking results.
  • Learned to triage urgent technical support requests while documenting others for follow-up.
  • Collaboration with supervisors and co-interns helped refine solutions and build confidence.
